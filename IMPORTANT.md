# IMPORTANT — read before coding in every session

## Preserve SACR v4.0 (section-aware reflection and lighting)

SACR is the Bridge's live transport algorithm, not a bloom or screen-capture effect. The original release choices and verification are in [Bridge Master v4](docs/BRIDGE-MASTER-V4.md) and its [receipt](research/master-v4/verification.json); the atmosphere now supplies the sky from the [v2 optical model](docs/BRIDGE-ATMOSPHERE.md). This page records the v4 contract independently of the look, renderer presets, or art assets.

1. **Addresses.** A section is X along the bridge, Y elevation (positive up in SACR, down in game drawing), Z across the deck. Convert at **48 world px / optical metre**; HUD travel uses a different conversion. `p.x` is measured from the left of the rolled view envelope, not from the player or canvas edge. Use `p.x + camWorldX` for the world address. Road depth runs from `SACR_NEAR_Z = 1.45` to `SACR_CURB_Z = 5.35`; actor lane is their midpoint (3.40). Camera, road, supports, rigs and emitters must agree on that address.
2. **Baked transport (A/B).** The angular sky, lunar flux and storm are independent of viewport pixels. Bake local section solids, canopy access and seven-sample finite-source visibility into RGBA8 (moon access / sky access / AO / storm access), linear RGB9_E5 practical radiance, and the two strongest visible light directions per vector cell (octahedral x/y + quantized energy). Keep facade and road rows separate. The rolling 1024×128 optical window, vectors, RGB9_E5 mips, curb/portal radiance and portal words must be staged and committed as a group. Keep the previous complete same-seed group until the next is ready; don't stretch or reuse an old seed's data. No synchronous readbacks or per-render CPU bakes.
3. **Live packet (C).** CPU `BridgeSACRFrame` packs actual pose capsules, weapon segments, signed-radius arcs, slab undersides, support elevations and sole contacts into a fixed **3,584-byte std140 block**. Offsets in bytes: column bins 0, primitive pool 640, architecture 1664, contacts 2176, shadow bins 2432, dynamic blockers 3072, supports 3456. Bin once into 80 X columns, at most 8 records (Master) or 4 (Optimized), using bounded horizontal ray dilation. Admit player/boss, opaque deck edges and important emission before filling spare slots with distant detail. No new uniforms inside this ABI without a packing/version migration.
4. **Evaluate a wet receiver.** If wetness < .04, return contact AO without tracing the curb atlas. Else intersect `t = max(0, curbZ - p.z) / max(.001, ray.z)`; use the world X and hit height at the curb. If the ray enters an authored aperture, trace to its back at the encoded depth. A ray leaving its lintel or jamb hits solid soffit; otherwise read the padded interior rows. Exterior rays read the exterior rows and the current sky. Evaluate only primitives in the receiver column, truncating horizontal displacement to the same dilation used in binning. Capsule distance is to a tapered segment; arc distance is to the clamped angular **centerline**, not a filled disk. Apply roughness-dependent feathering. Undersides stop or attenuate covered rays at their own depth. Max-union opaque coverage **per actor ID** before ordered transmittance, and accumulate emissive RGB separately; a blade or bolt never enters the opaque union or casts a shadow. Composite C plus transmitted B/sky, then apply wetness × Fresnel-like grazing gain × roughness response.
5. **Direct shadows are source-specific.** Contact AO is a separate bounded sole kernel using the real support height and lift. On road and raised decks admit the strongest eligible visible directions (2 on Master / 1 on Optimized), with a relative weight cutoff and smooth handoff. Project the incident ray to the actor lane and sample only opaque capsules in that shadow column. Remove energy only from the light that cast the shadow: lunar, practical, or the matched combat emitter. Do not multiply the whole scene or the emissive return by a shadow mask. Dry shelter tests, portal boundaries and wetness clipping must use the same section geometry.
6. **Light sources and receivers.** Admit explicit, bounded hit/arc/projectile/fixture records, never bright pixels, UI or full-screen flashes. Evaluate finite-support attenuation and test the light segment against opaque section blockers. The receiver's facing/normal and material response determine how much light it returns. Apply that contribution to the actor's existing contour, prop mask or shaded surface, not a halo painted over it. HDR emission and bloom are optional downstream presentation, not a substitute for incident light. Reuse the same sources and occluders across Master, Optimized and Canvas; cheaper paths may reduce samples and lobes, not change world geometry.
