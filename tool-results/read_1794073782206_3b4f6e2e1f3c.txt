     1→     1→/** STICKMAN RISING — the modular combo moveset, re-authored around REAL
     2→     2→ * swordsmanship (kenjutsu/iai techniques — see the per-attack notes).
     3→     3→ *
     4→     4→ * Design law for this revision:
     5→     5→ *  1. KAMAE CONTINUITY — every clip starts AND ends in chūdan-no-kamae (the
     6→     6→ *     middle guard). Chained attacks blend guard→chamber→cut→guard; the
     7→     7→ *     shared pose base means no snap between animation instances.
     8→     8→ *  2. HASUJI (blade alignment) — blade dir = π/2 − (shR+elR+wrR) in canvas
     9→     9→ *     space (+Y down), so: sum 1.57 → level forward · sum ≈ 3.0 → blade UP
    10→    10→ *     (jōdan) · sum ≈ 0.3 → blade DOWN (follow-through) · sum < 0 → blade

    12→    12→ *     kesa descends diagonally, gyaku-kesa rises, yoko stays level, kiri
    13→    13→ *     oroshi falls vertically, tsuki spears flat. NO 360° windmills.
    14→    14→ *  3. TEMPO + IN-BETWEENS — every fast span (>1.4 rad) carries an explicit
    15→    15→ *     intermediate "ignition" keyframe, so each frame is a real authored
    16→    16→ *     in-between pose: chamber → ignition → strike → follow → zanshin.
