    39→    39→# 1. RENDERING PARADIGM — 2.8D DEPTH-AWARE PERSPECTENCE
    40→    40→
    41→    41→## 1.1 Dimensional Definition
    42→    42→
    43→    43→This is NOT a flat 2D game. This is NOT a 3D game. This is **2.8D** — defined as follows:
    44→    44→
    45→    45→- **Gameplay plane:** All gameplay-critical movement, hitbox detection, collision, and combat mechanics operate on a 2D plane (Z = 0). Input is mapped to X (horizontal) and Y (vertical) axes on this plane.
    46→    46→- **Rendering pipeline:** The entire visual output is rendered using a 3D rendering pipeline with perspective projection. Characters, enemies, and key gameplay objects are 3D meshes rendered with perspective correction.
    47→    47→- **Depth layering:** The environment extends into Z-space across 6 distinct parallax layers (specified in Section 10). Each layer moves at a defined rate relative to camera motion.
    48→    48→- **Z-axis excursions:** Characters MAY move along the Z-axis during specific scripted sequences (cinematic dashes, finishers, dodge rolls). These excursions are limited to ±5 units from the combat plane, are camera-controlled, last no more than 1.5 seconds, and do NOT affect gameplay hitboxes (the character's combat hitbox remains locked to Z = 0 during the entire excursion).
    49→    49→- **Perspective correctness:** Objects closer to the camera render larger. The perspective FOV is 35° (vertical). Camera distance from the combat plane is 18 units. Objects at Z = 0 render at 1.0× scale. Objects at Z = -10 render at 0.78× scale. Objects at Z = +10 render at 1.35× scale.

    60→    60→- **Maximum simultaneous dynamic lights:** 32 (point lights), 8 (spot lights), 2 (directional)
    61→    61→- **Shadow maps:** Cascaded shadow maps for directional light (3 cascades: 512×512, 1024×1024, 2048×2048). Point light shadows: cubemap at 512×512 per light, maximum 8 simultaneous shadow-casting point lights.
    62→    62→- **Anti-aliasing:** TAA (Temporal Anti-Aliasing) at High quality, FXAA at Medium, none at Low
    63→    63→- **HDR rendering:** 16-bit floating point render target (RGBA16F) for proper bloom and exposure
