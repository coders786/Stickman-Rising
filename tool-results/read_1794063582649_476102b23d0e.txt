     1→     1→/** STICKMAN RISING — the modular combo moveset.
     2→     2→ *
     3→     3→ * Each attack = a keyframed clip (anticipation → strike → follow-through →
     4→     4→ * recovery) + hitbox window (the physical blade sweep is the hitbox) + trail
     5→     5→ * window + cancel window + legal follow-up chain. Directional inputs,
     6→     6→ * dash-cancels and buffers are resolved by the Player combo machine.
     7→     7→ *
     8→     8→ * Angle conventions (see pose.ts): arm angles measured from straight-down,
     9→     9→ * positive swings toward facing. Blade direction = π/2 − (shR+elR+wrR).
    10→    10→ */
