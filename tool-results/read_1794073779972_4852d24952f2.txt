     1→/** STICKMAN RISING — the modular combo moveset, re-authored around REAL
     2→ * swordsmanship (kenjutsu/iai techniques — see the per-attack notes).
     3→ *
     4→ * Design law for this revision:
     5→ *  1. KAMAE CONTINUITY — every clip starts AND ends in chūdan-no-kamae (the
     6→ *     middle guard). Chained attacks blend guard→chamber→cut→guard; the
     7→ *     shared pose base means no snap between animation instances.
     8→ *  2. HASUJI (blade alignment) — blade dir = π/2 − (shR+elR+wrR) in canvas
     9→ *     space (+Y down), so: sum 1.57 → level forward · sum ≈ 3.0 → blade UP
    10→ *     (jōdan) · sum ≈ 0.3 → blade DOWN (follow-through) · sum < 0 → blade
    11→ *     back (waki / sheathed). Cuts sweep through the plane they name:
    12→ *     kesa descends diagonally, gyaku-kesa rises, yoko stays level, kiri
    13→ *     oroshi falls vertically, tsuki spears flat. NO 360° windmills.
    14→ *  3. TEMPO + IN-BETWEENS — every fast span (>1.4 rad) carries an explicit
    15→ *     intermediate "ignition" keyframe, so each frame is a real authored
    16→ *     in-between pose: chamber → ignition → strike → follow → zanshin.
    17→ *  4. The blade is drawn from the SAYA (scabbard at the hip) for iai cuts
    18→ *     (l1, riposte): hand at the hip, blade lying back along the scabbard.
    19→ */
