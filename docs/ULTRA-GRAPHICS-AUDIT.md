# Ultra graphics: audit and overhaul plan

**Target:** a rain-dark transit viaduct above a believable night city. Three depths should read immediately: soft city and weather, crisp stage and fighters, near steel framing the action. Keep the stick-figure identity. Ultra should improve composition and material response, not simply brighten the same image.

This is a renderer/code audit with headless pixel checks, not a subjective score from live play or a GPU benchmark. Priority ranks severity to readability, not asset quality.

| Area | Assessment | Evidence / next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| City and horizon | **P0, layer-boundary defect** | The sky gradient and its backing met at fractional coordinates, while deep/far/mid plates added separate full-width dark fills. Fixed with an opaque backing, compatible sky endpoint, and feathered plate bases. Keep the lower district and its repeated floor join. |
| Asphalt and foreground | **P0, alignment defect; P1, material quality** | The capture mirror used a horizontal scanline even when the road rolled, could draw beyond the span, and captured any foreground dressing. Camera-aligned capture and clipping fix the ownership problem. Keep the structural girder and dashed paint: not every horizontal line is a bug. Next, reduce edge-paint prominence and add localized puddles instead of aiming for a uniformly glossy surface. |
| Weather | **P0, compositing defect** | The weather gel drew to an estimated horizontal curb over the finished scene. It now colors the background before the road and actors. Rain, lightning flash, and the vignette remain screen effects. |
| Scale and architecture | **P1, repetition risk** | The seeded plates repeat at fixed pitches, and variations in tint cannot hide repeated silhouettes. Build a library of roof profiles, signs, service levels and bridge landmarks. Give the skyline breathing room behind the fight lane. |
| Fighters and lighting | **P1, hierarchy risk** | Contact AO, cast shadows, and local probes already exist. Preserve that work; test limb and weapon readability against windows, rims and effects before increasing any light gains. |
| Ultra optics | **P1, inconsistency risk** | Forward optics and the capture/post route use different sources. The capture mirror remains a low-resolution approximation, with stepped slices; shimmer, discontinuous bands, and excessive glare merit device review. Prefer authored, surface-bound emissions over global glow. |
| UI and accessibility | **P2, validation gap** | Confirm the grade does not crush figures or wash out cues. Keep HUD outside post, and test reduced shake/flashes and combat indicators on all grades. |
| Frame pacing | **Release gate, unmeasured here** | The quality ladder, atlas ledger and sky cache already exist. Don't add a full-screen pass, canvas allocation, asset decode or readback to the steady-state frame without profiling and an explicit tradeoff. |

## Implementation sequence

1. **P0 — Correct depth and ground ownership (this patch).** Keep distant parallax independent, but give the road cull and reflection the full world-camera offset/roll. Capture the scene before near occluders and additive impact effects. Verify portrait opacity and a 2× capture beside each deck boundary. No changes to `ROAD.lane` or collision.
2. **P1 — Establish an art baseline.** Capture deterministic matched High/Ultra frames and 10-second clips: idle, pan along span, jump, crowded fight, hard hit/roll, lightning. Use at least three seeds (clear/hazy/wet), 1280×720 and 1920×1080 landscape, 390×844 portrait, DPR 1/2. Record effective tier and optics mode so an adaptive shed isn't misdiagnosed as bad art. Review at native size and in grayscale; use a three-value paint-over to decide where the darkest, calmest region belongs.
3. **P1 — Rebuild the stage material atlas.** Start with one span: mottled asphalt, sparse wet patches, bevel and grime on the front fascia, clean contact zone at the fighter's feet. Use retained seeded albedo, wetness and emissive masks; weather scales a bounded material response. Keep near props out of the central action window unless they intentionally occlude and pass readability review. Keep gameplay collision and geometry invariant across tiers.
4. **P1 — Recompose the city.** Author a handful of distinct silhouette families and landmark accents. Reduce high-contrast window density with distance; reserve saturated brights for sparse practicals. Batch-bake edge-safe tiles, preserve the lower district, and test wraps and resize. Match the influence of lamps on stage, characters and haze before adding new sources.
5. **P1 — Bring Ultra optics into agreement.** Use matched forward/capture frames as a reference. Introduce continuous wetness attenuation, soft reflection falloff and localized shaft scatter only where emitters justify them. Keep fill light and character rims subordinate to attack silhouettes. First prototype in Canvas2D with current budgets; a shader/WebGPU route is optional only if it wins a measured comparison and retains a Canvas fallback.
6. **P2 — Finish and release.** Playtest transitions between quality tiers, cold-start and reseed bakes, wide/tall viewports and low-contrast screens. Review the HUD and accessibility with post disabled and enabled. Publish matched captures and performance data with the release.

## Acceptance gates

- **Seams:** no clear or hard full-width row at a parallax baseline or weather transition; no mirror outside the visible road ends or past the front girder. Test roll in both directions, zoom, snap, DPR/resize and tile wraps. Intentional rails and curbs stay attached to the road.
- **Readability:** at gameplay speed and in grayscale, a reviewer can follow each fighter, foot contact, weapon and telegraph through the wet-night stress clip. A/B High vs Ultra should show improved depth and materials even with bloom disabled.
- **Performance targets (not measured results):** on a nominated desktop, aim for p95 frame interval ≤16.7ms at 1080p/60 Hz and ≤8.3ms on a separately nominated 120 Hz device. Record median, p95/p99, dropped frames, tier changes and ledger bytes over matched warm runs. Reject >10% p95 regression against the matched baseline or new sustained tier shedding. Lower tiers must retain stage coverage and gameplay cues.
- **Automation:** run `npm run gate`; keep the layer-seam, viaduct, lighting and Ultra tests. Add matched browser captures before marking the visual phases complete. Known unrelated baseline failures are in blade cleave, core strike joints, sword ritual keys, and unarmed strike contact.

## Implementation and release status

- **Stage and depth:** the Ultra material pass uses two retained quarter-scale plates, one for localized specular detail and one for capture-reflection attenuation. Both derive from the seeded road roughness field. Shoulder paint is less prominent, and the haze is feathered on both edges and drawn behind the road. The district now mixes three architectural profiles without changing floor repeat pitches. This is a procedural art pass, not a replacement of every skyline asset.
- **Optics:** capture reflections use a continuous soft lane-to-girder envelope and weather response. Forward actor reflections use the same roughness field with a local sample; forward and capture are intentionally not pixel-identical. The new pass adds no full-screen capture or per-frame canvas allocation. At the current road size the two plates together use about 126 KiB of RGBA backing data, tracked through the existing bake ledger.
- **Accessibility:** reduced-motion preference now also disables camera shake and the full-screen lightning overlay. Rain, weather state and in-world cues are preserved. The HUD still draws after scene optics.
- **Reference:** [Ultra viaduct still](lighting/ultra/viaduct-reference.png). Run `node --import tsx scripts/capture-ultra.ts` to regenerate 54 matched views (idle, pan, jump, crowd, impact and lightning × three seed/viewport setups × High capture, Ultra capture and Ultra forward). The files and timing manifest go into ignored `out/ultra-reference/`. Scenes are posed fixtures using the production renderer, not recordings of a full match. Manifest timings are software draw times and cannot certify browser/device FPS.
- **Release gates still open:** sustained browser motion clips and real-device p95/p99 benchmarks, in-motion readability review, and a hardware accessibility check. The code/test gate retains the same five known combat/animation failures reproduced on the original base.
