## Gravity sensitivity (ideal ballistic model)

- g=1350: upward 640 launch → apex 151.7px at 0.474s.
- g=1900: upward 640 launch → apex 107.8px at 0.337s.
- g=3250: upward 640 launch → apex 63.0px at 0.197s.

These ballistic figures ignore floor threshold, changing states, dt discretization and new juggle hits. They illustrate why consolidating duplicate gravity requires retuning, not why any particular gravity is correct.
