# Corrected mechanic: hold, then release into a horizontal leap

This supersedes HELD_SKIM_DASH.md and its hold-to-sustain mechanic. The old
held-skim captures are historical, not documentation of the current controls.

## Input and mechanics
- Forward/neutral grounded dash press enters `dashLoad`. No dash motor,
  cooldown/charge expenditure, invulnerability or fake travel during loading.
- Incoming movement brakes; a load pose progressively bends knees, inclines
  the trunk and gathers the hands. Hold as long as desired; charge caps at .65s.
- Release before .16s executes the normal grounded dash. Charge scales over
  .16–.55s; a longer hold does not automatically launch or maintain movement.
- Charged release consumes one normal dash and supplies a real takeoff velocity
  of -310 to -400 world pixels/sec. `grounded` becomes false; the regular finite
  DashBody force motor integrates horizontal acceleration and vertical gravity.
  No rendering-only suspension, frozen motor-age interval or hover extension.
- Core extends toward 1.52rad (~87deg from upright), arms sweep rearward,
  reachable IK trails the legs, and head follows the drive. Feet are allowed
  off the roof because the player really leaves it. Pelvis/foot-lock lowers for
  the streamlined silhouette. The flight extension fades before touchdown so
  the catch/recovery has time to happen.
- Release adds a ground push-off dust burst. Low ground wake/shadow streaks
  follow while near the roof. Landing uses normal impact/reseed feedback.
- Air, vertical, backward and high-speed momentum-reversal defensive dashes
  retain immediate input semantics rather than forcing a grounded charge.
- Existing cooldown, charge limits, equipment modes and attack cancels remain.
  The load is vulnerable, not another invulnerability window. Input reset on
  blur/pause cancels loading rather than accidentally firing on resume.

## What was actually studied
Downloaded and decoded the animated gameplay clip:
https://github.com/eguneys/celeste-jumping/blob/master/pre_dash_movement.gif
99 frames / 2.97 seconds; inspected 15 sequentially spaced frame samples.
This shows discrete departure/travel/fall transitions and clear airborne
separation. Its tiny arcade body is NOT a human anatomy or charge-pose reference.

Also downloaded the Naruto run animation:
https://github.com/DUNNIK/YoPlugin/blob/master/resources/Gifs/narutoRun.gif
Only 4 frames, 20x20 pixels, 400ms: a pixel run loop, not anime footage and not
reliable for detailed dash or launch timing. Previously inspected Naruto sprite
movement rows remain silhouette references only:

## Validation / actual output
- `bun run check`: 114 tests pass; typecheck, lint, animation/continuity audits pass.
- `bun run build`: successful. `git diff --check`: clean.
- Loaded-release integration tests at 30/60/120Hz × drawn/sheathed: no launch
  during .6s hold, actual negative physics y after release, near-horizontal
  pose, forward travel, floor clearance, return to ground and finite channels.
- Quick-release regression retains normal grounded dash. Input-reset regression
  cancels charge without firing. Existing opposing-momentum pivot test retained.
- Production browser: 420 updates at 120Hz / 210 captured frames at 60Hz,
  no JS errors, all poses finite, planted during charge, both equipment modes
  reach horizontal flight, no dash toe penetration. Minimum physics y ~-37.55
  world pixels; peak core pitch ~86.99 degrees. Capture precedes only the final
  blur/pause cancellation guard, which is separately regression tested.
- `release-leap-dash-gameplay.webp`: actual gameplay hold/release/flight/landing
  in both equipment modes. Offline animation capture, not a hardware FPS test.
- `release-leap-dash.png` and poses JSON: 12 real Player samples from hold to
  landing, generated by `node --import tsx scripts/render-release-dash.ts`.

This is stylized arcade flight with real ballistic displacement, not full
rigid-body balance/mocap or historical shinobi locomotion.
